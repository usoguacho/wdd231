Energy & Budget Tips

Small, low-cost changes that add up. Start with these, then request the full checklist below.

Seal drafts

Weatherstripping and door sweeps cost a few dollars and can noticeably cut heating and cooling loss.

Unplug idle electronics

Chargers and electronics draw power even when off. A power strip makes it a one-switch habit.

Track fixed vs. flexible spending

Separate bills you can't change from spending you can adjust — that's where real savings live.

Wash on cold

Most laundry gets just as clean on a cold cycle, and it avoids most of the energy cost of washing.
